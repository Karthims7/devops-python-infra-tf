# Woven by Toyota & Japanese Tech Ecosystem | Culture & Interview Guide

## 🚗 About Woven by Toyota
- **What is Woven by Toyota?**
  - Formerly known as **TRI-AD (Toyota Research Institute - Advanced Development)** and **Woven Planet Holdings**.
  - Headquartered in Nihonbashi, Tokyo, Japan.
  - It is Toyota's dedicated software arm building:
    1. **Arene OS:** Toyota's next-generation automotive operating system and vehicle software development platform (often called "the Android/iOS for cars").
    2. **Automated Driving & Safety:** Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicle technology.
    3. **Woven City:** A 175-acre smart city living laboratory prototype at the base of Mt. Fuji.
- **Engineering Philosophy:**
  - Blends Silicon Valley agile software engineering practices with the world-renowned **Toyota Production System (TPS)**.
  - Core values:
    - **Kaizen:** Relentless continuous improvement; eliminating toil and friction in engineering workflows.
    - **Genchi Genbutsu ("Go and see for yourself"):** Finding facts from real data/code rather than assumptions.
    - **Jidoka (Automation with a human touch):** Building quality into the process; stopping the line when a defect is detected.

## 🕒 India ⇄ Japan Time Zone Overlap (JST vs IST)
- **Time Difference:** Japan Standard Time (JST) is **3 hours and 30 minutes ahead** of Indian Standard Time (IST).
- **The JD Requirement:** *"Remote, with at least four hours of overlap with Japan working hours."*
- **Typical Workday Schedule:**
  - Japan standard work hours: **09:00 - 18:00 JST**
  - In Indian Time (IST): **05:30 - 14:30 IST**
  - A convenient 4-5 hour overlap window:
    - **07:30 IST to 14:30 IST** (equals **11:00 JST to 18:00 JST**).
  - During the interview, proactively mention:
    > *"I am very accustomed to working across international time zones. The 3.5 hour difference between India and Tokyo is very manageable. Starting my day at 7:30 AM or 8:00 AM IST gives us a seamless 5 to 6-hour daily overlap with the Tokyo team."*

## 💼 Contract & Rate Positioning (¥800,000 – ¥1,100,000 / month)
- **Currency Conversion Insight:**
  - ¥800,000/month ≈ **~₹4,40,000/month** ($5,300 USD)
  - ¥1,100,000/month ≈ **~₹6,10,000/month** ($7,300 USD)
  - Annual equivalent: **~¥9.6M – ¥13.2M JPY/year** (~₹53L – ₹73L INR/year).
- **Tax & Setup in India:**
  - As a B2B / Freelance foreign contractor from India, you will invoice Hays Japan / Woven by Toyota in JPY or USD via wire transfer (Inward Remittance with FIRC) or services like Wise/Payoneer/SWIFT.
  - In India, export of IT services is typically zero-rated under GST (with a Letter of Undertaking / LUT) and eligible for Section 44ADA (presumptive taxation) up to qualifying limits.
